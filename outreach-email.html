What is holding the current site back

It is slow on a phone

The site is built on Wix, which loads a lot of heavy code in the background. On a phone, where most of your clients are looking, that means slower pages. Slow pages quietly lose bookings, and Google notices the speed too.

Google struggles to read it

A lot of your text and services load after the page does, using JavaScript. Google does not always wait for that, so some of your content is harder for it to see and rank. Less of your work gets found.

It feels like a template

Page titles and descriptions look generic, the headings are not structured for search, and your treatments, courses and team are not laid out as proper, separate pages. There is no real structure for clients or for Google to follow.

Why it is not ranking on Google

Google likes sites it can read quickly, trust, and clearly tie to a local area. Right now a few things are working against the site:

Speed and stability scores

Google measures how fast and steady a page feels as it loads. Heavy Wix pages tend to score poorly here, and that pulls rankings down.

Weak on-page SEO

Thin or repeated page titles and descriptions, and none of the behind-the-scenes code (structured data) that lets Google show your prices, hours and reviews in search results. Google is not being told what each page is for.

Missing local signals

There is not enough on the page tying you to Stockport and the terms people actually search, like "lash technician Stockport", "makeup training Stockport" or "Stockport beauty salon". Your website and your Google Business listing should be saying the same thing.

Not much for Google to read

With services loading through JavaScript and no dedicated pages, there is little plain text for Google to index. Less content means fewer chances to show up.
