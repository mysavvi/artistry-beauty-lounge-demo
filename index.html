Welcome

Welcome to Artistry Beauty Lounge

We are a small team of highly qualified passionate women who want to empower each client who walks through our door. Our aim is to help you feel truly confident and provide the ultimate VIP experience.

We offer a range of luxury treatments which include Makeup Artistry, Hair Colouring, Hair Styling, Eyelash Extensions, Nails, Eyebrows and Lash Lifts. Whether you are looking for a natural subtle enhancement or a full glam transformation, we provide each service best suited to your individual preference. With over 11 years of experience in the beauty industry, we also offer a variety of accredited training courses to help you start your own career in beauty.
