Salon Owner

Alliyah

Meet Alliyah, our salon owner. Alliyah specialises in makeup artistry and natural beauty enhancements, with 11 years experience as a makeup artist offering services for bridal, shoots and all other occasions. Her natural beauty enhancements include eyelash extensions, lash lifting, eyebrows and hair. Alliyah is also an educator, offering fully accredited and non-accredited training courses to help you get started in the makeup and beauty industry.
